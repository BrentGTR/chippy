Why Parents & Kids Love Chippy

In Sweetville, Chippy the chocolate-chip cookie loves helping Leo the lion, Benny the bunny, and Ella the elephant. Putting everyone else first makes him happy, until he starts running on crumbs.

Written by a software test engineer & dad of a 4-year-old, Chippy reframes "quiet time" from a punishment ("Go to your room!") into a lifelong superpower.
